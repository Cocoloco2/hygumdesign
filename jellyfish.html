Kristoffer Hygum

Digital designer and maker. Focused on interaction design and prototyping.

I’m Kristoffer Hygum, a digital designer and maker with a master’s degree in Digital Transformation from Roskilde University.

My work explores how technology, design, and rapid prototyping can create new forms of interaction and experience.

I have a hands-on approach to design, often working with laser cutting, 3D printing, and fabrication tools to prototype and test ideas quickly.

I work as a technological consultant at EK, helping others bring creative and technical projects to life.

Jellyfish on Roskilde Festival

An interactive light installation exhibited at Roskilde Festival, exploring human touch and proximity through responsive light behavior.

Inspired by the movement and sensitivity of jellyfish, the piece created a shared sensory experience between humans and technology. My contribution combined digital fabrication and teaching support, helping turn a collaborative design into a physical installation.

Designing for a hectic and unpredictable environment like Roskilde Festival was an exciting challenge. It demanded quick thinking, hands-on experimentation, and the ability to balance artistic vision with practical constraints. The process taught me how to create something playful and engaging that could survive and thrive in a chaotic setting.
